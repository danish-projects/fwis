Features

What the system does today — grouped by how schools actually run.

School setupSchools, academic years, Sunday calendar, grades (1–6 Boys/Girls), teachers
StudentsGlobal student records — one person, many enrollments across years
EnrollmentsLink student to school, year, grade, and teacher for the season
AttendancePresent / Absent / Tardy per Sunday; consolidate matrix for admins
BehaviorNine levels adjust behavior score (starts at 100) on each enrollment
AssessmentsQuiz 1–5, midterm project, final exam in a classroom grid
Final gradesWeighted formula → letter grade, pass/fail, class rank
Access controlSuper Admin, School Admin, Teacher — scoped by role

Setup layer

1 Everything under a school is scoped to one academic year at a time.

EntityRelationshipExample
SchoolRoot of all dataHouston, Chicago, Dallas
Academic YearBelongs to one school2025–2026
CalendarBelongs to academic year — one row per SundayInstructional, Quiz 1–5, Holiday, Final
Grade / ClassroomBelongs to school — 12 per campusGrade 1 Boys
TeacherBelongs to school; assigned to one gradeGrade 1 Boys teacher

The calendar defines which Sundays count toward attendance % and which session type applies (quiz day, holiday, etc.).

Student & enrollment

2 Students are global; enrollments connect them to a specific school season and grade.

Student

Permanent record — name, gender, parent contact. Created once, never duplicated.

Enrollment

Joins student + school + academic year + grade + teacher. One student can have many enrollments (new year, transfer, promotion).
