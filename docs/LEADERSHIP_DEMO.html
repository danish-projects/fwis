Each school has its own calendar for the linked year — Houston and Chicago Sundays are separate. Those days define attendance % and session type (quiz day, holiday, etc.).

Security — in plain language

Think of student data like school records in a locked office. FWIS protects data on the way, at rest, and inside the app.

HTTPS (the lock in the browser)

Like a sealed envelope — nobody can read what you send while it travels. Login, grades, and attendance all use HTTPS.

Encryption in transit

Data is scrambled while moving between your computer and our servers (TLS). Same idea as HTTPS — safe on the road.

Encryption at rest

Sensitive papers stay in a locked filing cabinet. Student private details (DOB, parent phone, address) are encrypted in the database.

App security

Only staff with the right key open the right room. Sign-in required; each role sees only their school/grade; important actions are logged.

Input checks

We verify every form before saving — reject bad or suspicious entries. Login attempts are limited to slow down password guessing.

One-line summary

Protected on the way (HTTPS) · at rest (encrypted student PII) · inside the app (roles, login, validation).
